SCOPE, INPUTS & LIMITATIONS

Research decision support

SIREN forecasts daily Antarctic sea-ice concentration for the next 180 days and converts each field into destination- and ice-class-conditioned route guidance. It does not replace the judgment of a vessel master, ice navigator, or national ice service.
